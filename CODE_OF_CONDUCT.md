# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept feedback, correct mistakes, and help keep contributions accessible.
- Protect privacy. Use only synthetic, non-identifying data in public reports, examples, and evaluations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the current maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), using a non-public contact route published on that maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private route is available, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to provide one; do not include identities, screenshots, or other sensitive details in the issue.

The maintainer will review reports in confidence, avoid conflicts of interest where possible, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according to severity and recurrence. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation. No fixed response-time service level is promised.
